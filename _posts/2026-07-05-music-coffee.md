title: Dissonance Music is like Black Coffee

The dissonance in dissonant music is a lot like the bitterness in coffee.

When you're first exposed to it, it's overwhelmingly unpleasant; you can't believe that anyone would enjoy it.

But if it gets pushed on you even a little, you're exposed to it more and more...

The immediate harshness becomes less and less noticeable...

And in its place is a deeper understanding both of the bitterness itself, and how it actually enhances the other flavors.

You might even enjoy maximizing it by drinking black coffee.

Before you know it, you're wondering how people could not enjoy it, even being frustrated that they don't[^1]. 

You might feel like others aren't open or agentic enough to get past the barrier to greater enjoyment of things.

[^1]See the culture among black coffee enthusiasts, whose favorite pastime is doubting the sentience of non-black coffee enjoyers. Discussing if the people who need to load their coffee with cream, sugar, and sprinkles to consume coffee deserve to the gift of life. And they're kind of right to do so. I say this as someone who doesn't enjoy straight black coffee.
