Almost everyone who aspires to write music does so on their own initiative, not because a hand guided them to do so. The hand that guides people to other careers could be the promise of a high-paying job, a influence of a personal connection like a teacher or family, a desire for power, or a myriad of other selfish and less selfish reasons. With writing music, well, there isn't much promise in terms of pay, there aren't enough composers stalking around society for the average person to be likely to know one, and the average school board member has more influence than the average Curtis graduate will ever have. So most aspiring music writers are doing so on their own initiative and genuine passion for music, and you would think that self-selecting effect would make them, on average, good at writing music.
